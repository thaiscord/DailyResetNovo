## APP ICON DIRECTION

**Concept:** A soft golden sunrise or radial light — warmth, emergence, clarity.

**Design principles:**
- Warm cream/gold palette — matches in-app identity
- Minimal and symbolic — not a checklist or productivity icon
- The logo (existing) already communicates this beautifully
- Background: deep charcoal or very warm cream
- Symbol: the existing sunrise/reset icon — premium, timeless

**What to avoid:**
- Checkmarks or task icons
- Lightning bolts (productivity cliché)
- Brain icons (overcrowded market)
- Generic circles or rings

## REVIEW REQUEST STRATEGY

### When to ask (emotional triggers):
1. **Day 7 milestone** — first week completed — highest emotional state
2. **Day 30 milestone** — one month in — real commitment signal
3. **After viewing weekly recap** with score ≥ 5 days
4. **After comeback** — user returned after 3+ missed days AND completed 3 more days

### When NOT to ask:
- First 7 days
- After a missed day or low streak
- If asked in the last 30 days
- During the Reset Ritual

### Review moment design:
The review request should happen **after** an emotional milestone ceremony or recap — when the user is in a positive, reflective emotional state. Feels earned, not engineered.
